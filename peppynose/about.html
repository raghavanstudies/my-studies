Our story

Peppynose™ began in 2023 in Musiri, a small town near Trichy in Tamil Nadu, with a simple idea: the early years of parenthood are full of little moments, and each one deserves something made with care.

So we make simple, gentle products for those moments. A 2-in-1 sign that says “Bump on Board” while you’re expecting and flips to “Baby on Board” for the drive home. Soft cotton knee pads for first crawls. BPA-free silicone and neem wood teethers for first teeth. And toys that turn learning into play.

Every Peppynose product is sold on Amazon.in and Flipkart, so parents across India can order with the delivery and returns they already trust. We read every review, and we keep improving our products with what parents tell us.
