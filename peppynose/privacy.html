Summary

This website is an information site for Peppynose™ products. It has no user accounts, no shopping cart, no forms and no payments, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Information we receive

Emails you send us. If you write to care.peppynose@gmail.com, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

Website hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Fonts and photos from other services. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some photos from Pexels, so your browser connects to those services when a page opens.

Buying on Amazon.in and Flipkart

When you follow a link to Amazon.in or Flipkart, you leave this website. Your order, payment and delivery details are handled by that marketplace under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.
