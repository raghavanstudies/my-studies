Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about buying, delivery and our products.

Buying

Where can I buy Peppynose products?

On Amazon.in and Flipkart. Every product on this site has a button that opens it on the marketplace.

Who handles delivery, returns and refunds?

Amazon.in or Flipkart, depending on where you ordered, under that marketplace’s delivery and return policy. You can request a return from your order page there.

Why can’t I buy directly on this website?

We sell through Amazon.in and Flipkart so you get their fast delivery, secure payment and easy returns.

Car safety signs

What does 2-in-1 reversible mean?

One side says "Bump on Board" (or "Mom on Board") for pregnancy, and the other says "Baby on Board" for after your baby arrives. Just flip it.

What sizes are available?

14.5 × 14.5 cm, in packs of 1 or 2. A larger 17.5 × 17.5 cm size is coming soon.

How does it attach to the car?

With a suction cup on the rear windshield. It can be removed and moved to another car.

Teethers

From what age can my baby use the teethers?

From 3 months. The silicone is BPA-free and the shapes are neem wood.

What is in each teether combo?

Fish & Bird and Car & Cloud combos have 3 pieces each. The Complete Gift Set has 6 pieces.

Knee pads

What age are the knee pads for?

For little ones aged 1 to 3 years.

What are the knee pads made of?

Soft cotton. They protect knees and elbows while your baby crawls.

Which colours and packs are available?

Blue Smiley, Yellow Smiley, Gray Dotted and Black Dotted in packs of 2 or 4, and Mint Dotted in a pack of 4.

Toys

What age are the toys for?

The wooden kitchen set, alphabet and number foam mat and LCD writing tablets are for children aged 1 to 3 years.
